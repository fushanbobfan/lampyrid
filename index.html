Lampyrid

A meadow of fireflies, each flashing to its own rhythm and each nudging itself toward the flashes it sees. Turn up the coupling and watch the moment the meadow starts to pulse as one.

Click or drag on the meadow to startle a patch of fireflies out of step. Keys: Space pauses and resumes, R scatters every phase, S runs a sweep.

Sweep

Steps K from 0 to 4 on a separate copy of the current swarm, lets it settle at each step and averages r. The curve is the prediction for an infinite meadow; below the threshold a finite one still shows r of order 1/√N.
